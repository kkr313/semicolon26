1.	Protocol Summary

This protocol describes a study of CLN-201 in inflammatory disease. The study is designed to assess if the drug works and is safe.

Item	Description
Design	Randomized, blind placebo study, maybe open-label at some sites.
Population	Adults 18 to 70 or 75 years old.
Sample Size	100 or 120 patients depending on enrollment feasibility.
Treatment	CLN-201 10 mg once daily, possibly twice daily if not responding.
Control	Placebo / comparator not finalized.
Duration	10 weeks treatment, or 12 weeks, with follow-up 2 weeks maybe 4.
Primary Endpoint	Change in score at Week 8, maybe Week 12 if available.

2.	Introduction

CLN-201 may help inflammatory disease symptoms. More details will be added later.

3.	Objectives and Endpoints

Primary objective: improve symptoms
Secondary objectives: safety, labs, quality of life, biomarkers, other outcomes
 
The main endpoint is disease improvement using a score that will be defined elsewhere.

4.	Study Design

This is a multicenter study with blinded procedures where possible. Some study sites may use open-label operations due to logistics.
Participants may be randomized 1:1, although alternative allocation may be used if needed.

5.	Study Population

5.1	Inclusion Criteria

  Adults with inflammatory disease
  Stable medications maybe 4 weeks   Can sign consent

5.2	Exclusion Criteria

  Infection   Pregnant
  Heart issue
  Other exclusions to be added

6.	Treatment of Participants

Participants will receive CLN-201. Placebo or active comparator may be used depending on final regional strategy.
Dose may increase if participants do not respond.
