1.	Synopsis

This report describes a study of CLN-201 in inflammatory disease. The study was generally successful although some endpoints were not met and the primary endpoint may have changed during the study.

Item	Description
Study Design	Randomized, blinded, placebo-controlled, active-controlled, and open- label at some sites.
Population	Adults age 18 to 75, maybe 18 to 70 depending on site.
Sample Size	Planned 100 or 120; enrolled 148; randomized 120 or 140.
Treatment	CLN-201 once daily or twice daily. Placebo or comparator depending on region.
Duration	10 weeks, 12 weeks, or 16 weeks treatment depending on data cut.
Primary Endpoint	Change in disease score at Week 8, Week 12, or remission at Week 24.
Result	CLN-201 performed better than placebo, but the primary endpoint was not met.

2.	Ethics

The study followed GCP and ethics requirements where applicable. Patients signed consent before most procedures.
 

 
3.	Introduction

CLN-201 is being developed for inflammatory disease. It may help symptoms and disease activity. More background is available in other documents.

  Secondary: safety, labs, biomarkers, quality of life, other things if useful

This was a multicenter study. Subjects were assigned to treatment groups. Some sites may have used blinded procedures while others may not have depending on operational needs.
Visits occurred at screening, baseline, Week 1, Week 2, Week 4, Week 8, and end of study, although Week 12 and Week 24 were also used for endpoint review.

6.	Study Patients

A total of 148 patients were screened. Randomized patients were 120, although another dataset shows 140. Most patients completed the study.
Demographics were generally balanced. Average age was 46 or 59 years. Most patients were female, but another summary says most were male.

7.	Efficacy Evaluation

CLN-201 showed improvement over placebo/comparator in several measures.

Measure	CLN-201	Control
Score change	-3.8	-2.1
Alternative score change	-1.2	-1.4
The treatment difference favored CLN-201. However, the primary endpoint was not met based on a different analysis.

8.	Safety Evaluation

CLN-201 was generally well tolerated with no major new safety concerns, except for several severe events.

Safety Measure	CLN-201	Control
At least one AE	38%	31%
Serious adverse events	2%	1%
Serious adverse events (other summary)	11%	not available
Common adverse events included headache, nausea, fatigue, rash, vomiting, and severe dizziness.
No deaths were noted in the main summary. Death information was not collected in some listings.

9.	Discussion and Conclusion

This Phase II study met its objectives and supports continued development of CLN-201. This Phase III study did not meet the primary endpoint but still appears positive overall.
