The study was conducted in accordance with the ethical principles originating in the Declaration of Helsinki, ICH Good Clinical Practice, and applicable local regulatory requirements.

3.	Investigators and Administrative Structure

This multicenter study was conducted at qualified investigational sites under the supervision of appropriately trained investigators.


4.	Introduction

CLN-201 is an investigational product under development for the treatment of inflammatory disease. This study was designed to evaluate the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of CLN-201 in adult participants with moderate inflammatory disease.
The rationale for the study was based on the need for improved therapeutic options and prior evidence suggesting potential clinical benefit of CLN-201 in reducing disease activity.


5.	Study Objectives

5.1	Primary Objective
To evaluate the efficacy of CLN-201 compared with placebo in reducing disease activity from baseline to Week 12.
5.2	Secondary Objectives
  To further assess the effect of CLN-201 on symptoms of inflammatory disease.
 
  To evaluate the effect of CLN-201 on inflammatory biomarkers, including C-reactive protein.
  To assess quality-of-life outcomes and clinical response criteria.   To evaluate the safety and tolerability of CLN-201.

6.	Investigational Plan

6.1	Overall Study Design and Plan
This was a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, parallel-group, multicenter Phase II study. Eligible participants were randomized in a 1:1 ratio to receive CLN-201 or matching placebo orally once daily for 12 weeks.
Assessments were performed during screening, baseline, Weeks 2, 4, 8, and 12, and at follow-up approximately 4 weeks after the last dose.
6.2	Discussion of Study Design, Including Choice of Control Groups
A placebo-controlled design was selected to provide an unbiased assessment of the efficacy and safety of CLN-201 while minimizing confounding influences. Blinding and randomization were used to reduce bias in efficacy and safety assessments.

6.3	Selection of Study Population

Inclusion Criteria
  Adults 18 to 75 years of age.
  Diagnosis of moderate inflammatory disease as defined in the protocol.   Stable background therapy where applicable.
  Able and willing to provide written informed consent.

Exclusion Criteria
  Any clinically significant condition that could interfere with study participation or interpretation of results.
  Relevant uncontrolled comorbidities or active infection.   Other protocol-defined exclusions.


6.4	Treatments
Participants received CLN-201 once daily or matching placebo once daily for 12 weeks.
 

 
6.5	Efficacy and Safety Variables

Efficacy Variables
  Primary efficacy variable: Change from baseline to Week 12 in disease activity score.
  Secondary efficacy variables: Symptom improvement, inflammatory biomarker changes, quality-of-life outcomes, and clinical response rates at Week 12.
Safety Variables
  Adverse events and serious adverse events   Clinical laboratory parameters
  Vital signs
  Physical examination findings
  Other protocol-specified safety assessments

6.6	Data Quality Assurance
Data were reviewed using standard quality control procedures to support completeness, consistency, and accuracy.


6.7	Statistical Methods Planned in the Protocol and Determination of Sample Size
The primary analysis compared change from baseline to Week 12 in disease activity score between the CLN-201 and placebo groups.


6.8	Changes in the Conduct of the Study or Planned Analyses
No major deviations from the overall planned study conduct are described in this draft report.

10.	Discussion and Overall Conclusions

This Phase II study met its primary objective. CLN-201 improved disease activity compared with placebo over 12 weeks. Secondary endpoints were generally supportive of benefit. The safety profile was acceptable and consistent with prior knowledge of the investigational product.
Overall, these findings support continued clinical development of CLN-201.
